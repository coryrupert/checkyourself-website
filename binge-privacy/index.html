Privacy Policy

How Binge Yourself handles your information.

This policy describes how Binge Yourself (“the app”), published by Cory Rupert (“I”, “me”), handles information. It applies to the iPhone and iPad apps.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Who I am

Binge Yourself is an independent TV tracker. I do not operate my own servers for this app. Questions: coryrupert@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Binge Yourself stores information you create while using it, including:

  • Shows you add, and whether each one is Watching, Backlog, or Completed
  • Episodes you mark watched
  • Whether you want a reminder before a show airs
  • Your appearance choice and the country used for air dates

That information stays on your device. If you sign in with Trakt, the shows you follow and the episodes you mark watched are also sent to Trakt, as described below.

Trakt

Sign-in with Trakt is required so your watch progress can stay with your account. The app uses Trakt’s sign-in page. I do not receive your Trakt password.

After you sign in, the app stores your Trakt access token in the iOS Keychain on your device. It uses that token to read and update your Trakt watch history and watchlist. Trakt receives the shows and episodes you sync.

Trakt processes that information under Trakt’s privacy policy. You can sign out in the app, which removes the token from this device.

TVMaze

Show names, artwork, episode lists, and air dates come from TVMaze. When you search or open a show, the app sends that request to TVMaze, including the show name or id and, for schedules, the country you chose.

I do not add an account or an advertising identifier to those requests. TVMaze handles them under TVMaze’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders for a show, the app schedules a local notification on your device about one hour before an episode airs. Those reminders are optional, and you can turn them off for each show.

I do not send those notifications through a server, and they are not marketing messages.

What I do not collect

Binge Yourself does not:

  • Ask me for your name, email, or password
  • Use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs
  • Sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing

Your choices

  • Remove a show, or move it between Watching, Backlog, and Completed.
  • Turn reminders off for a show, or turn off notifications for Binge Yourself in iOS Settings.
  • Sign out of Trakt in the app.
  • Delete the app to remove the library stored on that device.

If you want help with a privacy request, email coryrupert@gmail.com.

Children

Binge Yourself is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will post the updated version at this URL and update the effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

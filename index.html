iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch

Check Yourself

Plan your workouts, check them off, and see your progress — with no ads and no tracking. Your plans stay on your devices, and sync over iCloud when you want them to.

Build a plan

Create your own routine or start from bundled plans for lifting, running, and mindfulness.

See the week

Home and Calendar show what’s today, what’s next, and what you already finished.

Check it off

Mark workouts complete, add notes, and watch trophies fill in as a plan gets done.

Keep it with you

Widgets on the Home Screen and a Watch app so you can update from wherever you train.

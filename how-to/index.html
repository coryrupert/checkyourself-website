Guide

How to use Check Yourself

Below is a basic user guide.

1

Pick a plan

Open the Plans tab. Bundled plans cover lifting, running, cycling, swimming and mindfulness. Tap a plan to see the details, then tap Apply Plan to put it on your calendar. You can turn on a daily reminder when you apply it.

2

See what’s assigned

Home shows Today, Upcoming, Recent, and In Progress Plans and Tasks. Tap the arrows to reorder the cards. Tap a workout to open it. Plus adds a Quick Task from any iPhone or iPad screen using the plus button.

3

Mark as Complete

On a workout, add notes or photos if you want, then tap Mark as Complete or Mark as Missed. Completed days get a check. Missed days stay in Recent and plan history.

4

Follow the calendar

Calendar is the full schedule. Use Today to jump back. Check off a day from the list, or open it for notes and photos.

5

Watch progress

Progress lists in-progress plans, Quick Tasks, and trophies for plans you finish. Open a plan to see history, filter remaining vs completed, edit remaining days, or turn the daily reminder on or off.

6

Make your own

Tap Add Plan to name the routine, pick an icon, and add each day’s details. Tap the moon for a rest day. You can also import a CSV.

On iOS 26, you can describe the plan instead and let Apple Intelligence fill in the days on your device.

7

Add a Quick Task

The plus on iPhone, iPad, and Home Screen widgets adds a one-off task with a due date and an optional reminder. Quick Tasks show up in Progress and on Apple Watch. They aren’t part of a training plan.

8

Add Home Screen widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Check Yourself.

Upcoming shows what’s next and can mark a workout complete. Progress shows how far you are through a plan. The plus on either widget opens Quick Add.

9

Use it from your wrist

On Apple Watch, swipe between Home, Progress, and Settings. Tap the circle next to a workout to mark it complete. Quick Tasks from iPhone or iPad show up here too.

10

Keep devices in sync

Turn on iCloud for Check Yourself in Settings. Plans, photos, Quick Tasks, and progress then sync across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

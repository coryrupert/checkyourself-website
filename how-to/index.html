How to use Check Yourself

Apply a plan, check off the day, or describe what you want and let Apple Intelligence build it. The same schedule stays on iPhone, iPad, Watch, and widgets.

1

Pick a plan

Open the Plans tab. Bundled plans cover lifting, running, cycling, and mindfulness. A sparkle on the icon means it was built or edited with Apple Intelligence. Tap a plan to see the days, then tap Apply Plan to put it on your calendar. You can turn on a daily reminder when you apply it.

2

Build with Apple Intelligence

On iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on, tap Add Plan, then Build with Apple Intelligence. Describe the plan and how long it should run. Day names are the workouts; the app already numbers the days.

You can also tap Edit with Apple Intelligence on a template, or on an in-progress plan from Progress, to change remaining days without rewriting everything by hand. Edits to an applied plan do not change the template on the Plans tab.

3

See what’s today

Home shows Today, Upcoming, Recent, and Progress. Tap the arrows in the corner to reorder those cards. Tap a workout to open it. The plus button adds a Quick Task from any screen.

4

Check it off

On a workout, add notes or photos if you want, then tap Mark as Complete or Mark as Missed. Completed days get a check. Missed days stay in Recent and plan history.

5

Follow the calendar

Calendar is the full schedule. Use Today to jump back. Check off a day from the list, or open it for notes and photos.

6

Watch progress

Progress lists in-progress plans, a Quick Tasks card, and trophies for plans you finish. Open a plan to see history, filter remaining vs completed, edit remaining days, or turn the daily reminder on or off.

7

Make your own

Tap Add Plan to build a routine: name, icon, daily or weekly repeat, and each day’s details. Tap the moon to mark a rest day. You can also import a CSV from the plan editor.

8

Add a Quick Task

The plus button on every screen — and on Apple Watch — adds a one-off task with a due date and an optional reminder. Quick Tasks show up in Progress and do not belong to a training plan.

9

Add Home Screen widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Check Yourself.

Upcoming shows what’s next and can mark a workout complete. Progress shows how far you are through a plan and opens the Progress tab.

10

Use it from your wrist

On Apple Watch, swipe between Home, Calendar, Progress, and Settings. Tap the plus button to add a Quick Task. Tap the circle next to a workout to mark it complete. Changes sync with iPhone and iPad over iCloud.

11

Keep devices in sync

Turn on iCloud for Check Yourself in Settings. Plans, photos, Quick Tasks, and progress then sync across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.
